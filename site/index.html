broker365

by Sultan Tech

broker365 is a small program you install and run on your own computer or server. It lets your personal AI agent (for example Hermes) work with your Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace accounts: files, spreadsheets, documents, presentations, mail, calendar, contacts and tasks.

How it works

  • You sign in to your Microsoft or Google account once, through the provider's own sign-in page.
  • The sign-in tokens are stored only on your machine, encrypted where the operating system allows it.
  • Your agent asks broker365 to do something, such as "create a spreadsheet". broker365 calls the Microsoft or Google API directly from your machine and returns the result to your agent.
  • broker365 has no server of its own. Sultan Tech never receives your files, messages, calendar data or tokens.

Who can use it

broker365 is privately distributed to people invited by Sultan Tech. It is not a public service.
